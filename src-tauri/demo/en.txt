attendees: Giulia Moretti, Luca Ferri, Anna Villa
tags: app-mobile
--- notes
@[Anna Villa] brings the support cases: technicians often work in basements and warehouses with no signal.

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
  participant T as Technician
  participant A as App
  participant S as Server
  T->>A: fills in the form offline
  A->>A: saves locally
  A-->>S: sends when the network is back
  S-->>A: confirms
```

::: matrix
| Criterion | Weight | Read only | Read and write |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use to technicians | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| Development time | 2 | 5 | 2 |
| Risk of conflicts | 2 | 5 | 2 |
:::
