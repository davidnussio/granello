---
title: Redact and review
description: Remove a sentence from a transcript for good, or mark it to review and keep the meeting away from online models until you decide.
---

Sometimes one sentence should not be in the transcript at all, or you are not sure yet. Select the text in the **Transcript** panel and a small bar appears with two commands.

## Redact

**Redact** deletes the selected text for good. It asks for confirmation, because it cannot be undone.

- The text is removed from the transcript and from search.
- It is never sent to any model and never shown again.
- The saved recordings of that meeting are deleted too, and so is any audio kept for online transcription: the words could still be heard there.

You can redact a whole passage or a few words inside a sentence.

## To review

**To review** flags the selected part. It stays readable, and a banner at the top of the transcript lists what is waiting.

While a meeting has parts to review:

- it is **not sent to online models**: no AI notes, no chat and no AI in the editor with an online provider, and no online transcription;
- the chat across all meetings leaves it out.

For each flagged part you then choose **Redact** or **Looks fine**. When nothing is left to review, the meeting works as before.

## When to use which

- You know it must go: **Redact**.
- You want to decide after the meeting, or ask someone first: **To review**, so nothing leaves the Mac in the meantime.
